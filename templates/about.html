Our Story

A bastion of refuge against injustice

The House of Laws was established to provide a fortress of refuge for persons of all classes, races and nationalities — rich or poor, Nigerian or non-Nigerian — against all forms of injustice, inequity, oppression and abuse of power.

Led and founded by Barr. JMCC Ogbuka, an egalitarian legal luminary and human rights activist, the firm has become renowned for its bold defence of equal rights, constitutional freedoms and the rule of law across Nigeria.

From our chambers in Enugu and Abuja, we combine intellectual rigour with practical advocacy to protect the rights of individuals, communities and organisations who seek justice.

Mission, Vision & Approach

Our Mission

To defend the fundamental and constitutional rights of every person, and to stand as a reliable counsel against injustice in all its forms.

Founder & Principal

Barr. JMCC Ogbuka

Egalitarian Legal Luminary · Human Rights Activist

Barr. JMCC Ogbuka is the founding force behind The House of Laws. His passion for legal erudition, bar advocacy and the protection of human rights has shaped the identity and direction of the firm.

Known for a radical yet principled approach to justice, he has consistently used the law as a tool for the advancement of equal rights, constitutional freedoms and public accountability.

  • Founder of The House of Laws (Cicero Chambers)
  • Leading advocate for fundamental human rights
  • Author and thought leader on justice and equality
  • Counsel in high-stakes constitutional and public interest matters
