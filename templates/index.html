About House of Laws

A fortress of justice for all

The House of Laws is established to provide a bastion of refuge for persons of all classes, races and nationalities against all forms of injustice, inequity, oppression and abuse of power.

Founded by Barr. JMCC Ogbuka, an egalitarian legal luminary and human rights activist, the firm is renowned for its bold defence of equal rights, constitutional freedoms and the rule of law across Nigeria.
